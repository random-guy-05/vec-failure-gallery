## Title
VEC Failure Mode Gallery - visual atlas of common prediction failures and metric symptoms

## What it is, in a line
Nine visual model/submission failure modes mapped to likely metric symptoms and debugging actions.

## What it is, and who it helps
A browser-friendly and printable visual atlas of nine synthetic Virtual Embryo failure modes: population collapse, wrong cell-state mixture, reversed gene response, scrambled co-expression, spatial line collapse, tissue scale error, local neighborhood scrambling, raw-count expression, and gene-order mismatch. Every plate shows a schematic “healthy vs failed” visual, the qualitative metric symptoms most likely to notice it, and a concrete next debugging action, while explicitly warning that signatures are diagnostic expectations rather than deterministic score guarantees. The gallery uses no Challenge targets or private data and ships as editable SVG, PNG, a combined PDF poster/deck, and a zero-install HTML gallery. It helps entrants recognize failure patterns quickly and connect model behavior to the official scoring questions.

## How someone else uses it
Open the repository and use the ready-made PNG/PDF assets, or edit the source SVG files. No Challenge data, sign-in, or Python environment is required to use the finished visuals.
